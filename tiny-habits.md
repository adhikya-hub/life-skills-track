## **2. Tiny Habits by BJ Fogg - Core Message**

### Question 2

**How can you use B = MAP to make making new habits easier? What are M, A and P.**

B = MAP means Behavior happens with Motivation, Ability, and Prompt.

- **M = Motivation:** desire to do something  
- **A = Ability:** how easy the habit is
- **P = Prompt** trigger to do the habit

## **4. Book Summary of Atomic Habits**

### Question 5

**What is the book's perspective about Identity?**

The book explains that lasting habits are built by changing identity. Instead of focusing only on goals, people should focus on becoming the type of person who naturally performs those habits.

---

### Question 6

**Write about the book's perspective on how to make a habit easier to do?**

The book suggests making habits easy by reducing friction and starting small. Preparing the environment, simplifying tasks, and using the “2-minute rule” helps building habits easier.

---

### Question 7

**Write about the book's perspective on how to make a habit harder to do?**

The book suggests increasing friction for bad habits by making them less convenient and less accessible. Removing triggers, adding obstacles, and making the habit time-consuming reduces the chances of repeating it.
