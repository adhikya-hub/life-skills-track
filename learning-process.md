# Learning Process

## Question 1

What is the Feynman Technique? Explain in 1 line.

The Feynman Technique is a learning method where you deeply understand a concept by trying to explain it in simple words as if you are teaching someone else.

## Question 3

What are active and diffused modes of thinking?

- **Focused Mode:** Its the mode where you think with concentration. Usually when actively solving problems or learning something difficult.
- **Diffused Mode:** A more relaxed thinking where the brain can be creative for deeper understanding.

## Question 5

What are some of the actions you can take going forward to improve your learning process?

- Reduce distractions while studying or coding.
- Practice deep work by focusing completely on one task at a time.
- Track my time and analyze where I waste attention.
- Try to explain concepts in my own words after learning them.
- Build small projects and implement concepts in code regularly.
- Communicate early whenever I face blockers or confusion.
- Set deadlines for assignments and practice drills.
- Revise concepts by teaching them to someone else or writing notes.
- Make learning enjoyable and stay curious while exploring topics.
