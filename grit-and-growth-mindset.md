# Grit and Growth Mindset

## **1. Grit**

### **Question 1**

**Paraphrase (summarize) the video in a few (1 or 2) lines. Use your own words.**

Grit is the ability to stay committed to long-term goals with passion and persistence, even while you are facing failure. Success depends more on consistent effort than natural talent.

---

## **2. Introduction to Growth Mindset**

### **Question 2**

**Paraphrase (summarize) the video in a few (1 or 2) lines in your own words.**

A growth mindset is the belief that skills and intelligence can be improved through learning, practice, and hard work. Mistakes and challenges should be seen as opportunities to grow, not signs of failure.

---

## **3. Understanding Internal Locus of Control**

### **Question 3**

**What is the Internal Locus of Control? What is the key point in the video?**

Internal Locus of Control means believing that your actions, decisions, and efforts are responsible for your success instead of blaming external situations or luck.

---

## **4. How to build a Growth Mindset**

### **Question 4**

**What are the key points mentioned by speaker to build growth mindset (explanation not needed).**
