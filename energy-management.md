# Energy Management

## Question 1: What are the activities you do that make you relax - Calm quadrant?

- Listening to music
- Taking a walk in nature
- Meditation or deep breathing
- Reading books
- Spending time with family and friends

## Question 2: When do you find getting into the Stress quadrant?

- Learning difficult concepts
- Tight deadlines
- Heavy workload
- Public speaking
- Lack of sleep

## Question 3: How do you understand if you are in the Excitement quadrant?

- Feeling energetic and motivated
- Feeling curious and engaged
- Losing track of time while working
- Feeling confident about challenges
- Wanting to learn and do more

## Question 4: Paraphrase the Sleep is your Superpower video in your own words in brief. Only the points, no explanation

- Sleep improves memory and learning
- Sleep strengthens the immune system
- Lack of sleep affects mental health
- Deep sleep helps physical recovery
- Consistent sleep improves overall brain function

## Question 5: What are some ideas that you can implement to sleep better?

- Maintain a fixed sleep schedule
- Avoid screens before bedtime
- Exercise regularly
- Keep the room dark and cool
- Practice meditation or deep breathing

## Question 6: Paraphrase the video - Brain Changing Benefits of Exercise. Minimum 5 points, only the points

- Exercise improves brain function
- Physical activity boosts memory
- Exercise increases focus and concentration
- Exercise reduces stress and anxiety
- Exercise improves mood and energy levels

## Question 7: What are some steps you can take to exercise more?

- Start with small daily workouts
- Take regular walks
- Follow a workout schedule
- Use stairs instead of elevators
- Choose physical activities you enjoy
