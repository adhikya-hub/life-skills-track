# Focus Management

## Question 1: What is Deep Work?

- Deep Work is the ability to focus without distractions on difficult tasks
- It helps in producing high-quality work in less time

## Question 2: According to the author how to do deep work properly, in a few points?

- Schedule dedicated time for focused work
- Work in a quiet and distraction-free environment
- Take proper breaks to maintain concentration

## Question 3: How can you implement the principles in your day to day life?

- Set fixed hours for focused work daily
- Avoid multitasking
- Use time blocks for important tasks
- Take short breaks between work sessions

## Question 4: What are the dangers of social media, in brief?

- Social media reduces attention span
- It creates addiction and distraction
- Excessive use increases stress and anxiety
- It wastes productive time
- Constant comparison affects mental health
