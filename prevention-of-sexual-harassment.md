# Prevention of Sexual Harassment

## 1. What kinds of behaviour cause sexual harassment?

Sexual harassment includes any unwelcome verbal, physical, non-verbal, or online behaviour of a sexual nature that creates an uncomfortable, hostile, unsafe, or intimidating work environment. According to the MountBlue PoSH policy, examples include:

- Inappropriate comments about a person’s appearance, body, or personal life
- Sexual jokes, gestures, remarks, innuendos, or sexist comments
- Unwelcome touching, brushing against someone, pinching, grabbing, kissing, or physical advances
- Staring, leering, whistling, or suggestive gestures
- Repeated unwanted requests for dates or outings
- Sending sexually offensive emails, texts, messages, images, or calls
- Displaying sexually explicit posters, pictures, or content
- Gender-based discrimination, stereotyping, or insulting remarks
- Abuse of authority that creates a hostile environment or denies equal opportunities based on gender
- Any behaviour that humiliates, intimidates, threatens, or affects a person’s dignity at work
- Harassment can happen between.

---

## 2. What would you do in case you face or witness any incident or repeated incidents of such behaviour?

- Clearly communicate that the behaviour is unwelcome and ask the person to stop, if it is safe to do so
- Maintain a record of incidents, including dates, time, location, messages, witnesses, and what happened
- Report the incident to the Internal Complaints Committee (ICC), HR, or the appropriate authority in writing
- Support the affected person and encourage them to report the issue, or do so myself with their consent
- Cooperate honestly during the investigation process while maintaining confidentiality
- In serious situations involving assault or threats, seek help from law enforcement authorities
